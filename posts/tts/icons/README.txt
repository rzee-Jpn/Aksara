Place your app icons here:
- icon-192.png (192x192)
- icon-512.png (512x512)

Quick generate with ImageMagick:
  magick -size 192x192 xc:#0d0d0f -fill '#d4a030' -font Helvetica-Bold -pointsize 72 -gravity Center -annotate 0 'TTS' icon-192.png
  magick -size 512x512 xc:#0d0d0f -fill '#d4a030' -font Helvetica-Bold -pointsize 180 -gravity Center -annotate 0 'TTS' icon-512.png
